# The Onboarding Experience (The "Magic Moment")

**Philosophy:** Traditional apps suffer from "onboarding fatigue" (asking for 15 fields of data before showing the user the app). Lynx takes the opposite approach. We want a frictionless, 15-second path to a massive dopamine hit.

## The 3-Step Flow

### Step 1: The Frictionless Auth (1 Click)
- **UI:** A completely black, minimalist screen. 
- **Typography:** The word "Lynx." in the center in a perfectly kerned serif font.
- **Action:** Only two buttons at the bottom: **"Continue with Apple"** and **"Continue with Google"**. No manual email/password entry, no email verification loops. 
- *Friction Level: Near Zero.*

### Step 2: The Core Identity (3 Fields)
- **UI:** The screen transitions smoothly. We do *not* ask for their phone number, email, address, LinkedIn, or even a headshot yet.
- **Action:** Just three massive, elegant text inputs:
  1. **Full Name**
  2. **Job Title (or Major if student)**
  3. **Company (or University)**
- **Button:** A sleek "Mint Card" button at the bottom.
- *Friction Level: ~10 seconds of typing.*

### Step 3: The Dopamine Hit (The "Aha!" Moment)
This is where the user gets hooked. As soon as they hit "Mint Card":
- **The Haptics:** The phone gives a heavy, satisfying physical vibration (using the Taptic Engine).
- **The Visuals:** The screen goes completely dark. Out of the shadows, a 3D digital card falls into the center of the screen with a satisfying "thud" animation. 
- **The Reveal:** It automatically applies the default "Bone" texture with raised lettering. Their Name and Title are beautifully embossed on the card in perfect typography. 

### Step 4: The Micro-Interaction
- They haven't even added any links yet, but they already feel like they own a premium status symbol.
- A subtle, glowing tooltip appears on the edge of the card: *"Swipe to flip"*
- When they swipe their thumb across the card, it physically rotates in 3D space with a soft light-glare effect passing over the texture. 
- The back of the card reveals a blank Master QR code and an empty App Grid with a glowing "+" button: *"Add your first link."*

## Why this works
By deferring all the heavy lifting (adding a phone number, uploading a resume, adding a GitHub link) to *after* they have seen the beautiful card, they are already emotionally invested in the product. They *want* to add their links because the card looks so good.
